# 0.14.0

Word 97-2003 `.doc` files can now be read as a full document model, not only
as main-story paragraph text. `readDocModel(bytes, limits?)` returns, for every
story (main text, headers and footers, footnotes, endnotes, comments and text
boxes), an ordered block tree:

- paragraphs with resolved paragraph and character properties (style cascade
  plus direct formatting), list references and inline items: text, line,
  page and column breaks, inline pictures with their image data, embedded
  OLE objects with their preview, field begin, instruction, separator and end
  marks with typed fields (HYPERLINK, REF, PAGEREF, TOC, PAGE, NUMPAGES, DATE,
  TIME, SEQ, MERGEFIELD, INCLUDEPICTURE), bookmarks, note and comment
  references, and floating shape and text box anchors;
- tables with their grid, horizontal and vertical merges, borders, shading,
  row heights, header rows and nested tables.

The model also carries sections (page size, orientation, margins, columns,
break type, title page, page numbering) with their header and footer
references and inheritance, footnotes, endnotes, comments with author,
initials and date, floating pictures and shapes with position and wrap,
styles, lists with their overrides, fonts, the stylesheet default fonts and
document properties. Pictures are returned as stored (PNG, JPEG, TIFF), DIB as
a BMP file, and EMF, WMF and PICT inflated.

The building blocks are exported too: `readDocFormatting`, `readDocStructure`
and `readDocObjects`, plus the shared sprm parser, stylesheet, list and font
readers. All readers are bounded by `DocModelLimits` and throw
`DocResourceLimitError` when a budget is exceeded; damage in an optional part
becomes a warning. See [the DOC model](../doc-model.md) and
[DOC objects](../doc-objects.md).

Not decoded yet: comment ranges (comments are anchored at their reference),
symbol-font characters, revision marks, form-field data, hyperlink binary
data, drawing shapes other than pictures and text boxes beyond their raw
OfficeArt properties, and paragraph properties stored in the Data stream.
Writing remains limited to the existing paragraph edits.

This release also contains the compatibility work described in
[compatibility coverage](../compatibility-increment.md): typed compound-file
storage operations, exact FILETIME round trips, XLS scalar editing and RK
promotion, and stricter DOC, PPT and VSD edit guards.
